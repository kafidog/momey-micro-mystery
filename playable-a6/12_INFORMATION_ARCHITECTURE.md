# Information Architecture

## Shared screen language

- common six-panel prologue;
- neutral event code and three same-seed role links;
- operator caption and recent-caption history;
- team diagnostic planning;
- final confirmed/unknown/decision summary;
- fixed consequence and one human question.

## Role screen language

- `【你的職責】`
- `【現在已知】`
- `【還不知道】`
- `【你可以做】`
- `【本輪只能查一項】`

## Diagnostic result

- `【查到的】`
- `【這代表】`
- `【仍不知道】`

## Consequence

- `【你們的決定】`
- `【接著發生】`
- `【後來確認】`

Voice carries short common narration and transitions. Screen cards carry durable facts and evidence boundaries. Humans carry interpretation, conflict, and decision. Information is divided by operational capability, never by loyalty or a rule against sharing.
