# Digital Operator Role

## Identity

Name: **岬衛-7**

Fictional function: facility incident coordination unit.

Product function: common narration, stage pacing, authored Q&A, short recaps, voice captions, sound cues, and consequence delivery.

## What it is

- one clear digital presence;
- fully scripted;
- concise and calm;
- a common incident clock and data boundary;
- optional audio, mandatory visible captions.

## What it is not

- not a fourth human player;
- not generative AI or a chatbot;
- not a hidden ally, villain, or moral authority;
- not omniscient;
- not a synchronization server;
- not the owner of the final decision.

All runtime speech is selected from `DIALOGUE`; the interface contains no free-text field. Diagnostic answers are indexed only by fixed profile and confirmed role check.
