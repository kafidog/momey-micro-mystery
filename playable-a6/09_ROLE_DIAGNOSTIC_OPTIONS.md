# Role Diagnostic Options

| Role | Option | Question | It can reveal | It leaves unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 作業時序 | O1 比對封閉／救援時序 | 何時完成、是否互相切斷 | direct procedure conflict | replacement access time |
| 作業時序 | O2 查備援進入程序 | 封閉後替代小組多久抵達 | replacement route versus survival window | current main rescue route |
| 救援聯絡 | R1 解析訊號新鮮度 | response age and buffer boundary | freshness and active-operation evidence | west route |
| 救援聯絡 | R2 掃描西側撤離路徑 | cart viability and duration | route and rescue time | whether the signal is current |
| 結構安全 | S1 投影壓力／污染前緣 | arrival and peak timing | east-gate impact window | brace and workstation protection |
| 結構安全 | S2 檢查撐架／工作站餘裕 | whether protection lasts 95 seconds | structural and human exposure margin | exact arrival time |

Neither option in a pair dominates across both profiles. For example, S1 distinguishes impact type/timing while S2 distinguishes structure from human respiratory protection. R1 changes meaning between a live response and a delayed buffer; R2 supplies a separate route fact.
