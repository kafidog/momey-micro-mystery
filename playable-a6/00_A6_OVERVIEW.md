# MOMEY PLAYABLE A6 — Overview

A6 is a static, same-room, three-human-player prototype. The three humans are peers: Operations/Timing, Rescue/Contact, and Safety/Structure. A fourth digital presence, 岬衛-7, handles common narration, authored prompts, pacing, captions, optional browser speech, restrained synthetic sound cues, and fixed consequences. It is not a player, generative AI, or decision-maker.

Core loop:

`SHARED PROLOGUE → SAME SEED → PEER ROLES → TEAM DIAGNOSTIC PLAN → ONE OF TWO CHECKS PER ROLE → SHARE ALL RESULTS → HUMAN FINAL DRAFT → SPOKEN AGREEMENT → CONFIRM → FIXED CONSEQUENCE`

Each play reveals three of six diagnostics. Two coherent incident profiles are deterministically derived from a shared URL seed. Queries reveal fixed truth; they never change it. No backend, account, analytics, speech recognition, LLM, or fake synchronization exists.
