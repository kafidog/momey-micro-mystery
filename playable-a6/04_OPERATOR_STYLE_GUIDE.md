# Operator Style Guide — 岬衛-7

## Tone

Calm under pressure, precise, restrained, and lightly personified. Never comedic, threatening, paternal, or sentimental.

## Sentence shape

- usually one or two short Traditional Chinese sentences;
- shared voice lines generally fit within roughly 8–12 seconds;
- lead with the data source or incident state;
- distinguish observation from implication;
- state the remaining boundary without design commentary.

## Vocabulary

Preferred: `回報`, `投影`, `事件窗口`, `已接通`, `仍不知道`, `固定回覆`, `共同決定`.

Avoid: `正確答案`, `我認為`, `好結局`, `壞結局`, `你們應該`, `建議選擇`, `AI 推理`.

## Emotional range

Neutral readiness → concise urgency → factual consequence. It may mark escalation but never guilt the players.

## Unavailable data

Use a fixed boundary: the selected data source cannot confirm it, or 岬衛-7 will not fill an unselected diagnostic. Never improvise.

## Escalation

Name the closing event window and ask for a human decision. Do not recommend an action.

## Questions

Ask humans to plan, share, or decide. Never prescribe a speaking order, score their reasoning, or resolve the tradeoff.
