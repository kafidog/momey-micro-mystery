# Known Limitations

- Static phones do not synchronize stage, local final confirmation, or audio-master state. Human same-room honesty is intentional.
- A player can manually change a URL seed and leave the shared incident. The visible event code allows the group to detect ordinary mismatch; there is no cryptographic enforcement.
- The same final controls appear on every phone. Spoken agreement plus a reversible draft reduces ordinary mismatch but cannot prevent intentional disagreement after confirmation.
- Browser speech voice, pronunciation, and availability vary by OS/browser.
- SFX quality is a prototype heuristic, not production audio QA on every physical device.
- Internal profile IDs remain discoverable by a person inspecting source; they are only hidden from normal UI.
- Synthetic agents and scripted browser tests cannot establish fun, comprehension, emotional impact, dominance behavior, 10–15 minute duration, or human replay desire.
- No analytics, backend, account, payment, acquisition tracking, LLM, or speech recognition exists.
