# A5 to A6 Design Decision

## What A5 preserved well

- three allied humans in the same room;
- a fixed incident truth and fixed consequences;
- open sharing with no deception or hidden loyalty;
- role-specific operational capabilities;
- local persistence, reset, mobile-first static delivery;
- a morally consequential final human choice.

## What A6 discards

- Command as host, verification authorizer, report custodian, final button owner, and consequence screen;
- the single global A/B verification;
- one fixed information path per replay;
- repeated human orchestration copy.

## What A6 adds

- 岬衛-7 as a fully authored digital orchestrator;
- three structurally peer human roles;
- one diagnostic slot per role with two mutually exclusive checks;
- two fixed incident profiles selected by a shared URL seed;
- caption-first speech synthesis and restrained WebAudio cues;
- local final draft, spoken-agreement gate, and explicit confirmation on every phone.

The six A5 storyboard images are intentionally reused byte-for-byte. A1–A5, root acquisition surfaces, Same-Room files, Worker, D1, and GA4 are unchanged.
