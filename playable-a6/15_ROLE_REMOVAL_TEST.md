# Role Removal Test

## Remove 作業時序

The table loses one diagnostic slot and the only direct view of procedure conflict or replacement-entry timing. Rescue and Safety cannot reconstruct whether closure cuts the rescue chain or whether alternate access fits the human window.

Result: decision quality materially reduced.

## Remove 救援聯絡

The table loses one diagnostic slot and the only credible P-side evidence about signal freshness, current route, and rescue duration.

Result: decision quality materially reduced.

## Remove 結構安全

The table loses one diagnostic slot and the only Q-side evidence about arrival, brace failure, or damaged respiratory protection.

Result: decision quality materially reduced.

Every role also owns a distinct responsibility and perspective. None owns orchestration or final authority. Synthetic structural test: PASS; real group behavior remains unverified.
