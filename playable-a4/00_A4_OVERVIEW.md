# MOMEY PLAYABLE A4 — Overview

A4 preserves A3's illustrated premise, three operational role tools, open factual sharing, compact board, one verification and four fixed consequence branches. Its new social loop is:

ROLE TOOL → CURRENT JUDGMENT → OPEN SHARING → COMPARE REASONS → VERIFY ONE UNKNOWN → RECONSIDER → SHARED COMMITMENT → CONSEQUENCE.

Each player records a local current judgment only after completing their own tool. The choices are Immediate Close, Maintain Rescue Window and Cannot Judge Yet. This is not a secret role, vote or final decision. After public sharing, the initial judgment becomes a local baseline. One verification may change it, leave it unchanged, or leave the player unsure.

The three phones remain independent static HTML/CSS/JS sessions. Human speech carries other players' facts and judgments; the interface never claims cross-phone synchronization.

Design intent remains three players, one phone each, same room, approximately 10–15 minutes. No backend, accounts, analytics, payment, AI or acquisition tracking is present.
