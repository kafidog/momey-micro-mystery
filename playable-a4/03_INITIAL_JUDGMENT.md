# Initial Judgment

PHONE DOES:

- unlocks only after the role tool has produced its evidence;
- asks what the player currently leans toward using only what they know now;
- offers Close, Hold and Cannot Judge Yet in one fixed order;
- lets the player change the local choice before open sharing is confirmed;
- locks the original baseline when open sharing is confirmed.

HUMAN DOES NEXT:

“I currently think ___ because ___.”

The selection is not secret, not a vote and not final. Players may quote it or show the phone. Unsure follows the same route as either directional judgment and does not disable verification or final commitment.

State fields are local to a seat: `initialJudgment`, `initialJudgmentAt` and `initialJudgmentLocked`.
