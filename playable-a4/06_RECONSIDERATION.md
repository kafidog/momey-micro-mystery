# Reconsideration

After the chosen verification result appears, each local phone shows the player's initial judgment and asks whether the result changed it.

Options:

- Changed
- Did not change
- I am still unsure

This is not another Close/Hold vote. It records only the relationship between new evidence and the earlier baseline. Any of the three answers can proceed.

The next prompt is one sentence: “Whose judgment changed this time, and why?” The final choice remains locked until the local player confirms that this short conversation happened.

PHONE DOES: makes a change of mind visible to the person who made the initial judgment.

HUMAN DOES NEXT: says why new evidence did or did not change the view.

State fields: `reconsideration`, `reconsiderationAt`, `postVerificationReady`, `postVerificationReadyAt`.
