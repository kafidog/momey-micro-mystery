# Known Limitations

- A4 is a static localStorage prototype; phones do not synchronize.
- A local readiness button records only that phone's user's spoken step.
- Initial judgments and reconsideration are gameplay state, not research data.
- Synthetic scenarios cannot prove real disagreement, equal participation, pacing or fun.
- Exact physical devices, assistive technologies and browsers outside the tested matrix are unverified.
- The story uses fixed fictional evidence and outcomes, not live sensors.
- No F1, F2, Momey-specific willingness-to-pay, E3 or E4 evidence exists.
