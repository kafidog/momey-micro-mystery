# Full-Sharing Stress Test

Assumption: at the earliest allowed point, all three players reveal every fact, show their evidence and state their current judgments.

Result:

- Command still contributes the causal time conflict.
- Rescue still contributes the credible person-side signal and its freshness/route boundary.
- Safety still contributes the downstream physical path and brace uncertainty.
- Full sharing leaves two explicit unknowns.
- Only one unknown can be verified.
- The unverified side remains in the final summary.
- Initial judgments can remain different because responsibility and risk weighting differ even when facts are common.
- The phone never patches the design by prohibiting quotation, showing or sharing.

Rendered evidence used three isolated browser contexts. Command, Rescue and Safety openly shared all relevant information, locked different initial judgments (Unsure / Hold / Close), selected the same A verification and reached the same Close commitment without any synchronization or withholding rule.

FULL_SHARING_STRESS_TEST = PASS.
