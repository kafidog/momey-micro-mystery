# A3 → A4 Design Decision

## Council finding

A3 made every role operate a different instrument, but the resulting exchange was still mainly a transfer of facts. A4 uses each role's incomplete evidence and professional responsibility to produce a defensible personal judgment before the table combines its lenses.

## Material changes

- Added one short local current-judgment step after own evidence.
- Added a genuine Unsure option without penalty.
- Locked the initial judgment only after local confirmation of open sharing.
- Reframed verification as choosing which unresolved unknown matters most to the disagreement or hesitation.
- Added one short post-verification reconsideration and one natural discussion gate.
- Kept the final choice locked until that reflection has returned to human conversation.
- Replaced the consequence handoff prompt with one reflection question.

## Deliberately unchanged

Story, art, names, timeline, fixed truth, three role tools, compact factual board, all-facts-shareable rule, A/B verification, Close/Hold branches, consequences, visual foundation and static architecture are unchanged.

## Remaining unknown

Synthetic structure cannot prove that real groups will disagree naturally, resist an alpha player, finish in 10–15 minutes or find the experience fun.
