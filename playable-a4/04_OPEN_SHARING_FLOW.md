# Open Sharing Flow

After every player has a current judgment, the interface gives one compact discussion target:

“Put the three findings, current judgments and reasons together. Which unknown is actually blocking you?”

All relevant facts, evidence cards and judgments may be spoken, quoted or shown. There is no hidden-loyalty rule and no prohibition on full sharing.

Each local phone confirms only that its user has participated in the spoken exchange. It does not assert that the other devices are ready. The shared-looking board is a fixed three-line factual memory aid:

1. Command: 20-second closure cuts off 95-second rescue.
2. Rescue: credible response; freshness/location/route remain unknown.
3. Safety: pressure reaches the east gate at 95 seconds; brace endurance remains unknown.

No vote total or other players' judgments are rendered. The local phone may remind its owner of only that owner's initial judgment.
