# Information Architecture

| Stage | Phone shows | Human does next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Common premise | Six frozen panels and common accident facts | Selects one professional lens |
| 02 Role tool | One causal instrument | Operates, then interprets |
| 03 Evidence / current judgment | Own evidence plus Close/Hold/Unsure | States a current direction and reason |
| 04 Open sharing | One target, factual three-line board, own baseline | Shares all facts and compares reasons |
| 05 Two unknowns | A west-side unknown and B east-gate unknown | Identifies what blocks agreement |
| 06 Verification | Draft/change/confirm/lock/result | Chooses which uncertainty matters more |
| 07 Reconsideration | Own baseline and changed/unchanged/unsure | Explains what changed and why |
| 08 Final | Confirmed / unknown / must decide | Commits as one team |
| 09 Consequence | Decision / next / later confirmed | Reflects once without scoring |

No stage reads other phones' local state. The compact board contains facts only.
