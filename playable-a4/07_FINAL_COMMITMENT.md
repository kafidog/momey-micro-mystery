# Final Commitment

The final stage unlocks only after:

- own tool and evidence;
- initial judgment;
- open sharing confirmation;
- one locked verification;
- local reconsideration;
- short post-verification discussion confirmation.

It preserves:

【已確認】 what the role tools and selected verification established.

【仍未知】 the unverified side and any residual causal uncertainty.

【現在必須決定】 Immediate Close or Maintain 95 Seconds.

The group may discuss freely before one local draft is confirmed. The decision is not scored or morally labelled. Both result branches preserve the same fixed truth used in A3.
