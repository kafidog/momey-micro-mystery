# Perspective / Judgment Architecture

| Role | Pre-sharing lens | Defensible Close reason | Defensible Hold reason | Defensible Unsure reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command | 20-second closure conflicts with 95-second rescue | Preserve the fast system-wide closure path while downstream endurance is unknown | Closure certainly destroys the only known rescue window | Timeline alone cannot resolve which human risk should lead |
| Rescue | Credible deliberate response, but freshness and route are unknown | A possibly stale signal and unknown route may not justify exposing the other side | The signal preserves a credible living-person possibility that closure ends | Credibility, freshness and reachability do not resolve into one direction |
| Safety | Pressure reaches Gao Cheng at 95 seconds; brace limit unknown | Waiting exposes a known person at the downstream brace | Exact endurance is still unknown while Lin Rui may remain rescuable | Projection proves arrival, not the brace's final survival time |

These are design-audit possibilities, not player instructions. Runtime copy never tells a role which option it should select.

Facts and judgment are rendered in separate sections. Facts use Source, Time, Content and Cannot Yet Confirm. Judgment uses a restrained local choice panel with equal order and styling on every phone.
