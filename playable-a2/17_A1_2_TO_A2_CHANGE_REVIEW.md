# A1.2 to A2 Change Review

## Ownership and preservation

`playable-a1/**` remained byte-for-byte untouched during this worker run. A2 is an isolated `playable-a2/` route and does not add a link from root, `sr-h1`, `sr-h2`, `sr-h3`, Threads or Portaly.

## Design changes

| A1.2 reference pattern | A2 decision |
| --- | --- |
| Gameplay begins close to information cards | Start with six common illustrated panels and causal captions |
| Seat-specific information can feel like separate clue cards | Each role has shared situation, duty, known, unknown and unique operational action |
| Asymmetry may imply withholding | Explicit same-team open sharing; asymmetry comes from capability and evidence source |
| Choice/verification can be interpreted as a quiz | A/B cards show equal `可以知道`/`仍不知道`, with local draft/confirm/lock |
| Abstract P/Q naming | 林芮、高承、海岬防洪站、西側維修隧道、東閘手動撐點 |
| Result can be a conclusion label | Stable evidence card leaves interpretation to players, then objective consequence |

## Not changed

No root scripts/styles, same-room counters, worker/D1, GA4, account, acquisition or public delivery state was touched. No claim is made that A2 supersedes A1.2 until Sol High approves.
