# Human Coordination as Shared State

The three phones are intentionally independent. The shared state is carried by the people saying:

- all evidence and current leanings have been shared;
- the team selected A or B;
- the chosen specialist completed the check and reported it;
- the team agreed on Close or Hold.

The UI never claims cross-device synchronization. This is an intentional same-room architecture, not a hidden technical workaround.
