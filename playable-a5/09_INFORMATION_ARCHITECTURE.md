# Information Architecture

A5 preserves the aligned format:

- Role: responsibility, known, unknown, capability.
- Evidence: source, time, content, cannot-yet-confirm.
- Personal perspective: one current Close/Hold/Unsure judgment.
- Command verification: one A/B authorization.
- Final: confirmed, unknown, now decide.
- Consequence: decision, immediate event, later confirmation.

All relevant facts may be spoken or shown. Information is separated by operational capability, not secrecy or loyalty.
