# A4 to A5 Design Decision

A4 proved that three perspectives, open sharing, limited verification, and a fixed consequence matrix work. Its weakness was duplicated local group workflow: every phone confirmed sharing, selected verification, recorded reconsideration, selected the final action, and rendered a consequence.

A5 removes those duplicated forms. It deliberately keeps only one explicit individual input per player: Close, Hold, or Unsure after their role evidence. Group coordination moves to spoken same-room exchange, while Command holds procedural authorization and execution controls.

The story, art, factual topology, tools, verification facts, and consequence truth are unchanged.
