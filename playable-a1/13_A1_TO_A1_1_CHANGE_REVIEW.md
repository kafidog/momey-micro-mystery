# A1 to A1.1 change review

## What was weak in A1

A1 was technically playable, but its cards still sounded like an incident-management report. Stage 3 gave the key reinterpretation before players had to discover it together. The downstream cost was described as an abstract operational risk rather than a person who had to remain in a dangerous position.

## What changed

A1.1 compresses player-facing cards into plain Traditional Chinese, adds Q as the downstream manual-isolation operator seeded before commitment, and changes Stage 3 to an ambiguous status followed by a discussion gate and a separately revealed system definition. A asks whether the signal is really from P; B asks whether waiting brings danger to Q. Stage 5 gives 人員訊號席、封鎖風險席、事件指揮席 private responsibilities, then lets the group discuss freely before identical local commitments.

## Why it changed

The revision keeps the fixed truth and local static architecture while moving the work from phone-led recitation to human explanation, questioning, and tradeoff. Q makes the same delay window legible as a human cost: possible irreversible bodily harm is seeded before commitment, while the fixed permanent respiratory injury and removal from field duty are revealed only after commitment.

## What Sol High believes is materially better

The intended improvement is a shorter, clearer social loop: each player has a reason to speak, the central reframe must be articulated before the phone confirms it, both P and Q are present before the decision, and neither verification nor commitment is presented as a morality quiz. The delay branch is now concrete and irreversible without making immediate sealing a painless obvious answer.

## What still cannot be known without humans

Local synthetic QA cannot establish whether real groups find the language natural, whether Q creates the intended emotional weight, whether the free discussion actually resists an alpha player, whether the session is fun or comfortable, or whether anyone would host/repeat/pay. Those remain unproven.
