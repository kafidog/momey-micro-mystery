# A1.1 to A1.2 change review

## Why this bounded pass exists

The A1.1 foundation was playable but still had three coherence risks: player-facing copy was long and partly meta, the Stage 3 definition lived in the initial DOM, and the information split made Q repeat as an abstract stake instead of a person doing second-door work. Stage 4 also treated the first click as both choice and reveal.

## What changed

1. Player-facing entry and seat copy was rewritten without changing the three-person/same-room/one-phone/static/seven-stage foundation. The same word-like metric moved from S1=915, S2=920, S3=876 to final S1=674, S2=687, S3=703.
2. A1.1 metadata, fairness explanation, and author moral commentary were removed from the player UI. Practical private-screen and local-state instructions remain.
3. Stage 3 now places only the neutral `0 已確認人員` task in the initial HTML. The confirmation-vs-physical-truth definition is created after the shared-interpretation gate and persists through a refresh at the discovery stage.
4. S1 now owns command/timeline and only Q's station. S2 owns P/human signal without detailed Q bodily harm. S3 owns physical movement, why Q cannot leave, timing, possible severity, and human consequence. Q is written as a person working the second door.
5. Stage 4 now has `verificationDraft` and `verificationConfirmed`: A/B are editable before confirmation, results stay hidden, the group must answer `三人都確認選同一項了嗎？`, the confirmed choice locks, and reset clears both fields.
6. Stage 5 now gives each seat one short private prompt, then leaves the conversation free while retaining the final local commitment gate.
7. All review docs, tests, source snapshot, and screenshot index are versioned as A1.2. The old A1→A1.1 artifact is replaced by this file.

## Fixed truth decision

`01_FIXED_TRUTH.md` and the P/Q branch map remain unchanged in substance. Before commitment, the possible irreversible Q harm is known; the fixed permanent respiratory injury and field-duty consequence remain after a delayed commitment. No A/B branch changes that truth.
