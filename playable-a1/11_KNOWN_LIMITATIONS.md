# Known limitations — A1.2

- There is no network synchronization. A/B draft alignment and identical final commitment are human coordination, not server-enforced state.
- Local storage is browser-profile local. A new browser/device starts at Stage 0; an old state with a draft but no `verificationConfirmed` cannot show a result.
- The prototype does not authenticate, prevent intentional screen sharing, or provide cryptographic secrecy.
- Q's fixed irreversible outcome is intentionally shown only after commitment; the person, station, causal delay, time window, and possibility of irreversible bodily harm are known beforehand.
- The entry and seat UI does not present score, win/loss, or author/fairness commentary. This does not prove that real groups will read the tradeoff as comfortable or balanced.
- Static QA cannot establish actual fun, social comfort, repeat hosting, WTP, E3, or E4.
- Browser screenshots and synthetic roleplay, if captured, are technical evidence only; they are not human feedback.
