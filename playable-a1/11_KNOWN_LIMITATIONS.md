# Known limitations — A1.1

- There is no network synchronization; identical final commitment is a human agreement, not a server-enforced state.
- Local storage is browser-profile local. A new browser/device starts at Stage 0; an old A1 state without `recontextConfirmed` is held at the discovery prompt until the new definition is opened.
- The prototype does not authenticate, prevent intentional screen sharing, or provide cryptographic secrecy.
- No facilitator is required after start, but players must read short prompts, speak in their own words, and discuss honestly.
- Q's fixed irreversible outcome is intentionally shown only after commitment; the person, station, causal delay, and possibility of irreversible bodily harm are known beforehand.
- Consequence text is fixed and deliberately avoids a correct moral answer.
- The static QA pass cannot establish actual fun, social comfort, repeat hosting, WTP, E3, or E4.
- Browser screenshots and synthetic roleplay, if captured, are technical evidence only; they are not human feedback.
