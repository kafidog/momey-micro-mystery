# Stage flow

| Stage | Phone does | Humans do | Exit condition |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 — 準備 | States the room/device contract and role. | Sit together and agree to keep screens private. | Each seat starts its own briefing. |
| 1 — 私密 | Shows one role-specific fact. | Read, look up, and explain the first necessary fact. | The seat has spoken its starting point. |
| 2 — 交換 | Shows the second role-specific fact; Seat 1 seeds the no-confirmed-exit timeline before recontextualization. | Listen, connect, and explain the second contribution. | The group has exchanged all three perspectives and U1/U4/U2/U3 are seeded. |
| 3 — 重構 | Shows the shared `0 confirmed personnel` interpretation boundary without introducing a new key fact. | Ask what the state proves and does not prove. | All three can state the difference between confirmation and physical truth. |
| 4 — 查證 | Offers exactly A or B; reveals a seat-specific fragment for the selected result. | Agree verbally on one uncertainty to verify, then share all three fragments. | Each seat has shared its selected fragment and moves on; the unselected option remains credible but unverified. |
| 5 — 共識 | Presents two commitment buttons, the three-round oral protocol, and a local confirmation gate. | Seat 2 states the human loss; Seat 3 states the downstream cost; Seat 1 restates both without deciding; then all select identically. | Each phone confirms its local commitment. |
| 6 — 後果 | Shows the fixed consequence and selected-verification interpretation. | Discuss what was protected and what was sacrificed. | Complete end; deliberate reset is available. |

## Pacing intent

The first private unit is one click from the entry state and appears before any long rulebook. There are ten meaningful units in the matrix, with two required contributions per seat. Every phone interaction is followed by a reason to look up and speak.
