# Stage flow — A1.2

| Stage | Phone does | Humans do | Exit condition |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 — 準備 | States the room/device contract and role. | Sit together and keep screens private. | Each seat starts its own local view. |
| 1 — 私密 | Shows one short role-specific fact. | Read, look up, and explain the first fact in their own words. | The seat has spoken its starting point. |
| 2 — 交換 | Shows the second role-specific fact. | Listen, connect, ask a question, and explain the second contribution. | The group has exchanged the six necessary pre-Stage-3 units. |
| 3 — 發現 | First shows only `0 已確認人員` and a neutral task. The definition container is empty until the gate. | Describe what the line does and does not let them say, without a phone-supplied definition. | Shared-interpretation checkbox, then explicit definition reveal; `recontextConfirmed` persists. |
| 4 — 查證 | Offers A/B as an editable local draft. Result cards remain hidden. | Agree verbally on one question, select it on every phone, check `三人都確認選同一項了嗎？`, and confirm. | Confirmed choice locks; one seat-specific fragment appears. |
| 5 — 共識 | Shows one short private prompt and two commitment buttons. | Discuss freely; each phone records the same commitment after the agreement checkbox. | Local commitment confirmation. |
| 6 — 後果 | Shows the fixed consequence and the selected-verification interpretation. | Discuss who was protected and who bore the cost; restart only deliberately. | Complete end. |

## Human-first sequence

The phone stages an ambiguous line, waits for human interpretation, and then supplies the definition. It does not synchronize the A/B draft or the final commitment; each local confirmation records what the group has already agreed aloud.

## Pacing and copy

The first private unit is one click from entry. The same metric used for the baseline gives S1=674, S2=687, and S3=703 word-like segments after the final correction. All are below 800 and below the 750 warning threshold.
