# Stage flow — A1.1

| Stage | Phone does | Humans do | Exit condition |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 — 準備 | States the room/device contract and role. | Sit together and agree to keep screens private. | Each seat starts its own private view. |
| 1 — 私密 | Shows one short role-specific fact. | Read, look up, and explain the first fact in their own words. | The seat has spoken its starting point. |
| 2 — 交換 | Shows a second role-specific fact; each seat returns a necessary contribution and Q is seeded before any ending. | Listen, connect, ask a question, and explain the second contribution. | The group has exchanged U1/U4/U2/U5/U3/U6. |
| 3 — 發現 | First shows only `0 已確認人員`; a checkbox and control appear, but the definition is hidden. | Use their own words to interpret the first two cards and ask what the line leaves open. | After the group checks that it has talked, the control reveals the definition; only then can Stage 4 open. |
| 4 — 查證 | Offers exactly A or B in plain language and reveals one short seat-specific fragment. | Agree verbally on one question, choose it on every phone, then explain the fragment to the other seats. | Each seat has shared its selected fragment. |
| 5 — 共識 | Shows a private responsibility and two commitment buttons; no scripted three-round checklist. | 人員訊號席 states the P-side cost, 封鎖風險席 states Q's cost, 事件指揮席 neutrally restates the unresolved tradeoff; the group discusses freely and agrees. | Each phone records the identical local commitment after the agreement checkbox. |
| 6 — 後果 | Shows the fixed consequence and the selected-verification interpretation. | Discuss who was protected and who bore the cost; restart only deliberately. | Complete end. |

## Human-first sequence

The phone does not solve the central reframe. It stages an ambiguous line, waits for a group exchange, and then provides a definition. The phone does not synchronize the commitment. It records the choice after humans have discussed and agreed.

## Pacing intent

The first private unit is one click from the entry state. Cards are short and high-signal; the social work is explaining, asking, challenging, and deciding. Each seat has two necessary pre-commitment contributions and at least one Q reference before the commitment.
