## Seat dependency

- Without S1, the group lacks lawful entry, no-exit timing, command window, and the first Q reference.
- Without S2, the group lacks the human/P possibility and cannot argue for preserving the rescue window.
- Without S3, the group lacks the physical/Q danger and cannot articulate the cost of waiting.
- Q is referenced by more than one seat before commitment; Q is a person in the causal chain, not an ending surprise or a balance patch.
- No single seat contains both the P possibility and the Q consequence.
