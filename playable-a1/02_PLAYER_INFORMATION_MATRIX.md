## Seat dependency

- Without S1, the group cannot establish lawful entry, service-failure timing, or who can issue the command.
- Without S2, the group cannot assess whether the human-origin signal deserves attention.
- Without S3, the group cannot assess the real downstream cost of delay.
- No single seat contains both the human possibility and the containment consequence.
